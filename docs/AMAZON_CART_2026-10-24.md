## Notes

1. **Dowel length.** Amazon US has no hardened 4 × 36 pin, only 4 × 40 and stainless shelf pins. Stainless is too soft here: the limit-case bending stress is 604 MPa. Press the 40 mm pin 6.5 mm into the base as modelled. It then stands 4.5 mm above the cover top. The O1 pins sit at y = ±51 and the O2 pins at x = 32.3, both outside the controller board, and a model check found nothing in the way. Grind the pins flush if you prefer.
2. **Shoulder screws.** These are 304 stainless rather than 12.9 steel. The double-shear stress at the 200 N limit is 21.6 MPa, so the safety factor drops from 46 to 12. The structural table is updated.
3. **Battery.** No single 650 mAh 3S XT30 pack from a known brand shipped fast, so this is the TATTU 2-pack. The 18 W charger delivers about 1.4 A into a 3S pack, about 2C for 650 mAh. Check the charge rating on the pack label before using it, and charge at 1C if the label says so.
4. **Column-end screw.** The kit has M5 × 14 but no × 16. An × 14 through the 8 mm base leaves 6 mm of thread in the tapped column end, which is enough. With the desk clamps, the 4 bench screws are not needed.
